1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Username, email address (if you create an account)
  • Profile Information: Avatar, display name, preferences
  • Communication: Messages you send to our support team

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers
  • Game Play Data: Game progress, scores, achievements, in-game purchases
  • Usage Information: Session duration, features used, crashes, performance data
  • Location Data: Approximate location based on IP address (not precise GPS)

1.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Social Media: If you connect via Facebook, Google, or other social platforms
  • Advertising Partners: Ad performance and interaction data
  • Payment Processors: Purchase confirmation (but not payment card details)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and Improve Services: Operate, maintain, and enhance our games
  • Personalization: Customize your gaming experience and recommendations
  • Customer Support: Respond to your questions and provide technical support
  • Analytics: Understand how players use our games and identify areas for improvement
  • Communication: Send you updates, promotional offers, and important notifications
  • Security: Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
  • Legal Compliance: Comply with applicable laws and regulations
  • Advertising: Display relevant advertisements and measure ad performance

3. How We Share Your Information

We may share your information with:

3.1 Service Providers

  • Cloud hosting providers
  • Analytics services (Google Analytics, Firebase)
  • Advertising networks (AdMob, Unity Ads)
  • Customer support platforms
  • Payment processors

3.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to valid legal requests.

3.3 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the new entity.

Important: We do NOT sell your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

4. Children's Privacy

We are committed to protecting children's privacy. Our games may be suitable for children, and we comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations.

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent
  • If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly
  • Parents can contact us to review, delete, or stop further collection of their child's information
  • We use age-appropriate ad content for games targeted at children

Parents: If you believe your child has provided information to us, please contact us at privacy@universeinvedors.com

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Secure cloud infrastructure
  • Employee training on data protection

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 Access and Correction

You have the right to access and update your personal information.

6.2 Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and personal data by contacting us.

6.3 Opt-Out of Marketing

You can opt-out of promotional communications at any time.

6.4 Ad Preferences

You can limit personalized advertising through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy → Advertising → Limit Ad Tracking

6.5 Do Not Track

Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" feature. We currently do not respond to these signals.

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations:

To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@universeinvedors.com

  • Right to object to processing
  • Right to withdraw consent
